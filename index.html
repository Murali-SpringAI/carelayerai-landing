Current Research: Cashless Claims & Pre-Authorization

Hospital insurance teams often coordinate information across claim forms, discharge summaries, bills, laboratory reports, diagnostic reports, and policy documents. When information is incomplete or inconsistent, the result can be repeated payer queries, additional documentation requests, discharge delays, and manual rework.

Our Approach

CareLayer AI is exploring how an evidence intelligence layer can work with existing hospital and payer systems to identify documentation and evidence issues before submission, connect clinical and financial information, and support faster human review without replacing the reviewer.

🏥
Cashless Claims & Revenue Cycle Reduce documentation gaps, payer queries, claim rework, billing inconsistencies, and discharge delays.
🧾
Pre-Authorization Readiness Identify missing or inconsistent information before authorization requests reach the payer.
⚙️
Evidence Intelligence Connect diagnoses, treatments, labs, discharge documentation, bills, and policy information into one evidence view.

Healthcare Interoperability Integrate intelligent workflows with existing hospital and payer systems using standards such as FHIR.
